Privacy Policy

App: StarLeeway (Android)  |  Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oleksandr Boiko ("Developer", "we", "us") handles information in the mobile game StarLeeway ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store personal data about players.

The App contains third-party advertising SDKs. These SDKs may automatically collect and process the following data on our behalf or on their own behalf:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen settings, and app version;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location);
  • Ad interaction data, such as ad impressions, clicks, and performance and diagnostic information.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. How we use information

Information handled through the App's SDKs is used for the following purposes:

  • displaying advertisements and measuring their performance (advertising or marketing);
  • preventing ad fraud and invalid traffic, and ensuring security;
  • maintaining and improving the stability and performance of the App.

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money.

4. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID / opt out of ad personalization: on your Android device go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may differ slightly depending on the manufacturer).
  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland), the App requests your consent for ad-related data processing, and you may change your choice where the App provides this option.
  • Uninstall: you can stop all data collection by the App by uninstalling it.

5. Data sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except with the advertising service providers listed above, as needed to show ads, and where required by law.

6. Data retention and security

We do not keep personal data about players on our own servers. Retention of data by third-party SDK providers is governed by their respective privacy policies. Data is transmitted over the internet using industry-standard protocols, but no method of transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will help to address the matter.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we do not store personal data about players ourselves, most requests should be directed to the relevant ad service providers listed above. You may also contact us at the email address below, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
